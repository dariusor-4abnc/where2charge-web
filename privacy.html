Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Where2Charge is made in Singapore by 4A Business and Consultancy ("4ABnC", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Where2Charge app (on the web at app.where2charge.4abnc.com, and on Android and iPhone) and this website, how we use it, and your choices, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

In short

  • You can browse chargers, availability and parking fees without an account.
  • Your location is used on your device to show nearby chargers. We don't keep a location history.
  • We don't collect payment details, and we don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

  • Account (only if you sign in): your email address, the name from your Google or Apple account if you sign in with one, and an account ID. Sign-in is needed to save chargers, write reviews, add prices or chargers, and report or confirm parking fees.
  • Location: with your permission, your device's location is used in the app to centre the map, show distances and find chargers near you. It is not sent to us or stored, except the map position of a charger you choose to add.
  • What you contribute: reviews and ratings, prices, chargers you add, parking fee reports (with an optional photo of the rate sign), your answers to "Is this parking fee right?", reports about reviews, and feedback messages.
  • Saved chargers: the chargers you bookmark.
  • Search: places you search for, which are looked up to move the map.
  • Website analytics: this website (where2charge.4abnc.com) uses Google Analytics to count visits and clicks, which sets cookies. The web version of the app (app.where2charge.4abnc.com) uses Google Analytics to count visits and which features are used, such as filters and directions. This sets cookies. It does not receive your location or account details. The iPhone and Android apps do not use Google Analytics. Google Analytics only runs if you accept analytics cookies when asked. You can change your choice at any time under Cookie settings: at the bottom of this website's pages, or in the web app under Account.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: show chargers, availability, parking fees and distances, and keep your saved chargers.
  • To show your reviews, prices and charger additions to other drivers.
  • To keep parking fees accurate, using your reports and confirmations.
  • To moderate content: reported reviews are checked within 24 hours, and new reviews pass a word filter.
  • To reply to your feedback and keep the service secure.

3. Service providers

  • Google Cloud and Firebase: hosting, database and sign-in.
  • Google and Apple: if you choose to sign in with them.
  • OneMap (Singapore Land Authority): map tiles and place search.
  • Google Analytics: visit statistics for this website and the web version of the app.

Charger and car park data come from public sources such as LTA DataMall and data.gov.sg; no personal data is sent to them. We don't sell personal data. We may disclose it where the law requires.

4. Keeping and deleting data

  • We keep your account data while your account exists.
  • Delete your account in the app: Account → Delete my account. This removes your account, profile, saved chargers, reviews and feedback.
  • Parking fee reports and chargers you added help other drivers and may be kept after you delete your account. Contact us to have them removed.

5. Security and overseas transfers

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with Google Cloud, which may process it outside Singapore under protections comparable to the PDPA. Access to the admin tools is restricted to authorised staff.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Commission.

7. Children

Where2Charge is meant for drivers and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
