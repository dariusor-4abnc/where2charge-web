Charging prices and parking

Know the charging price and parking rate, where we have them

Charging and parking are often billed separately. Where2Charge shows both side by side when the information is available, and tells you when it isn't.

Charging prices

  • Published prices are shown per kWh, for each connector.
  • Some prices are added by drivers and labelled as community prices.
  • Not every charger has a price yet. When a price is missing, none is shown; it is never displayed as $0 or as free charging.

Parking information

  • "Parking now" shows the rate for the current Singapore time, with its source, such as "Official HDB rate".
  • Rates come from public HDB, LTA, JTC and URA data, plus driver reports.
  • Coverage isn't complete. If we can't find or read a rate, the app says "Parking fee unknown". If the car park match is uncertain, it asks you to check the signs on site.

Checked by drivers

Answer "Is this parking fee right?" or report a different fee with a photo of the rate sign. The "Free parking" filter only includes car parks whose rate data says parking is free right now. An unknown rate is never counted as free.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I check HDB EV charger availability?

You can check live HDB EV charger availability instantly on Where2Charge. Our map shows which charging points and bays are open in HDB carparks across Singapore, so you know before you drive.

Which Singapore EV charging networks do you support?

Where2Charge currently supports live availability tracking for charging points on major networks including SP Mobility, Charge+, CDG Engie, and HDB carpark chargers.

How do I find an EV charger nearby?

Open the Where2Charge map, then search a place or use your location. Charging points are listed nearest first, and you can turn on “Available now” to hide occupied bays and filter by connector type and speed to see only the ports your car can use.

How do I find a free EV charger in Singapore?

It depends what “free” means. If you want a charger that is free to use right now, meaning not occupied, turn on “Available now” on the Where2Charge map to see only ports with a free connector. If you want charging at no cost, note that public EV charging points in Singapore are generally priced per kWh. Where2Charge shows the published price for each connector, and when a price is missing it is never shown as $0 or free. To save on parking, use the “Free parking” filter, which only includes carparks whose rate data says parking is free right now.

Is Where2Charge free to use?

Yes, Where2Charge is completely free to use. There are no logins required to find EV charging locations, check which ports are available or compare charging prices.
