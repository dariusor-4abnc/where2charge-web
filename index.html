New in Where2Charge

The parking fee, not just the charging fee

Charging is only half the bill. Each charger shows the fee of the car park it's in, at the current Singapore time.

  • HDB, mall, hotel and JTC car parks. Rates from public data, with public holidays counted as Sundays.

  • Free parking filter. Show only chargers where parking is free right now.

  • Checked by drivers. Answer "Is this parking fee right?" or report a new fee with a photo of the rate sign. Each fee shows when it was last updated or confirmed.
