How it works

    Find a charger

Charger discovery

Show only the chargers that fit your car

Public chargers across Singapore, listed from LTA DataMall, on one map. Narrow them down in a few taps.

  • Available now only. Hide chargers that are busy, offline or have no current status.

  • AC or DC, and connector. Type 2, Combo 2 (CCS2) or CHAdeMO.

  • Minimum charging speed. 22, 50 or 150 kW and up.

  • Distance. Within 2, 5 or 10 km of you or the place you searched.

Status comes from operators' public data and is refreshed regularly, not second by second. The app shows when it was last updated and marks chargers that have no current status.

Nearby alternatives

First choice taken? Your backup is a tap away

Bays fill up. Before you set off, or when you arrive to find yours occupied, check what else is close by.

  1. 1

    Search where you're going, or use your location. The list sorts by nearest to that spot.

  2. 2

  3. 3

Charging prices and parking

Know the charging price and parking rate, where we have them

Charging and parking are often billed separately. Where2Charge shows both side by side when the information is available, and tells you when it isn't.

Charging prices

  • Published prices are shown per kWh, for each connector.
  • Some prices are added by drivers and labelled as community prices.
  • Not every charger has a price yet. When a price is missing, none is shown; it is never displayed as $0 or as free charging.

Parking information

  • "Parking now" shows the rate for the current Singapore time, with its source, such as "Official HDB rate".
  • Rates come from public HDB, LTA, JTC and URA data, plus driver reports.
  • Coverage isn't complete. If we can't find or read a rate, the app says "Parking fee unknown". If the car park match is uncertain, it asks you to check the signs on site.

Checked by drivers

Answer "Is this parking fee right?" or report a different fee with a photo of the rate sign. The "Free parking" filter only includes car parks whose rate data says parking is free right now. An unknown rate is never counted as free.

Built with drivers

Reviews, saved chargers and your feedback

Browsing needs no account. Sign in when you want to save chargers or share what you found.

  • Driver reviews. Rate a charger and add a photo. Was it working, easy to find, worth the trip? Reviews can be reported in the app, and reports are checked within 24 hours.

  • Saved chargers. Keep your regular spots in the Saved tab, and filter the map to show only them.

  • Fill the gaps. Add a missing charger or a price, and confirm or report parking fees, so the next driver knows.
