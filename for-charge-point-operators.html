How to list EV chargers on the map

Already listed

Public chargers are already listed from LTA DataMall, so drivers can find many locations today.

OCPI 2.2.1 integration

Share availability and tariffs directly through OCPI 2.2.1, so drivers see current status and prices where you publish them.

Talk to us

Message our integration team on WhatsApp and we will go through your setup and next steps.

Why share your charger data

  • Drivers searching for a charger can see which of your connectors are available.
  • Published tariffs are shown per kWh for each connector.
  • Drivers who find a bay occupied can be directed to another compatible charger nearby.
  • No cost to drivers: Where2Charge is free to use, with no sign-up needed to browse.
